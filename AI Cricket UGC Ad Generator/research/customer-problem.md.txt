# Customer Problem Identification — SG Thunder Striker English Willow Cricket Bat

## Product

SG Thunder Striker English Willow Cricket Bat

## Campaign Sector

Cricket

## Target Audience

Young and amateur cricket players

---

# Possible Customer Problems

## Problem 1 — Selected

"I'm progressing in cricket, but I'm unsure whether my current bat is right for the next level of my game."

### Why It Matters

As players become more serious about cricket, they may start paying more attention to equipment factors such as:

- Feel
- Balance
- Control
- Bat profile
- Size
- Weight
- Sweet spot
- Overall suitability

This creates a natural reason for the player to research and consider a different cricket bat.

### Emotional Angle

**Progression + Ambition**

The player feels that their cricket is developing and they want their equipment choices to reflect that progression.

---

## Problem 2

"I don't know what features actually matter when choosing my next cricket bat."

### Why It Matters

Young and amateur players may see many different cricket bats and may not understand the differences between profiles, sweet spots, balance, edges and other features.

### Emotional Angle

**Confusion + Desire for Confidence**

---

## Problem 3

"I'm confused about which bat profile and balance would suit my cricket."

### Why It Matters

Players may struggle to understand whether a particular bat's profile or balance is appropriate for their playing style and level.

### Emotional Angle

**Uncertainty + Decision Making**

---

## Problem 4

"There are so many cricket bats available that choosing one feels difficult."

### Why It Matters

The number of cricket bat options can make purchasing decisions more complicated, especially for younger or less experienced players.

### Emotional Angle

**Choice Overload + Need for Guidance**

---

## Problem 5

"I want to take cricket more seriously, but I'm not sure what kind of bat I should consider next."

### Why It Matters

Players who are progressing may start researching better-suited equipment as part of their cricket journey.

### Emotional Angle

**Aspiration + Progression**

---

# Selected Customer Problem

## Primary Problem

> "I'm progressing in cricket, but I'm unsure whether my current bat is right for the next level of my game."

---

# Why This Problem Was Selected

This problem was selected because it:

1. Is relatable to developing cricket players.
2. Naturally connects with the idea of choosing a next cricket bat.
3. Creates a strong opening for UGC storytelling.
4. Supports the campaign theme **"My Next Step in Cricket."**
5. Allows the product to be introduced naturally.
6. Does not require unsupported performance claims.
7. Works well within a short-form advertisement.

---

# Emotional Angle

## Progression + Ambition

The player is no longer treating cricket as just a casual activity.

They are becoming more serious about the game and are starting to think carefully about their equipment choices.
