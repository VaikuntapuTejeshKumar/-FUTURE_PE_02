# Target Audience Profile — SG Thunder Striker English Willow Cricket Bat

## Product

SG Thunder Striker English Willow Cricket Bat

## Campaign Sector

Cricket

## Primary Target Audience

Young and amateur cricket players who are progressing in their cricket journey and are becoming more serious about their game.

---

## 1. Age Range

### Primary Campaign Audience

Approximately 12–18 years.

This is a campaign audience assumption based on the product positioning and intended content strategy, not an official product age specification.

---

## 2. Cricket Experience Level

The campaign primarily targets:

- Developing cricket players
- Amateur players
- Intermediate players
- School cricket players
- College cricket players
- Academy players
- Young players participating in local cricket

These players are moving beyond casual cricket and are becoming more interested in choosing equipment carefully.

---

## 3. Typical Playing Environment

The target audience may play cricket in:

- School grounds
- College grounds
- Cricket academies
- Practice nets
- Local clubs
- Community grounds
- Training sessions
- Youth matches
- Weekend cricket games

---

## 4. Main Cricket-Related Goals

The audience may want to:

- Improve their cricket skills
- Become more consistent
- Take cricket more seriously
- Progress to a higher playing level
- Build confidence
- Choose suitable cricket equipment
- Find a bat that matches their developing game
- Improve their overall cricket experience

These are audience motivations and should not be presented as guaranteed results of using the product.

---

## 5. Problems They Face When Choosing a Cricket Bat

Common concerns include:

- Choosing the correct bat size
- Understanding bat weight
- Finding suitable balance
- Understanding different bat profiles
- Deciding between different willow types
- Comparing cricket bats
- Understanding whether a bat suits their playing level
- Finding good value
- Knowing which features actually matter
- Deciding whether their current bat is still suitable

---

## 6. What They Look For in a Cricket Bat

The audience may pay attention to:

- English Willow
- Bat profile
- Sweet spot
- Balance
- Weight
- Handle design
- Edge profile
- Blade shape
- Overall appearance
- Size
- Price/value
- Brand reputation

---

## 7. Common Doubts Before Purchasing

Potential questions include:

- Is this bat suitable for me?
- Is the size right for me?
- Is the weight manageable?
- How does the bat feel?
- Is the balance suitable?
- What type of player is it designed for?
- Is English Willow the right choice for me?
- Is the product worth considering?
- How does it compare with other bats?
- Does it suit my next stage of cricket?

---

## 8. Emotional Motivations

The major emotional motivations include:

### Progression

The player wants to feel that they are moving forward in cricket.

### Confidence

They want equipment that makes them feel prepared and serious.

### Ambition

They want to improve and potentially reach a higher level of cricket.

### Identity

Cricket is becoming an important part of their identity and lifestyle.

### Excitement

Choosing a new cricket bat creates excitement around practice and matches.

- Facebook

---

## 11. Suitable UGC Content Angle

### Main Angle

**"My Next Step in Cricket"**

The content should show a young cricket player becoming more serious about the game and starting to think more carefully about their cricket equipment.

The product can be introduced as an option worth considering.

---

## 12. UGC Communication Style

The communication should be:

- Authentic
- Relatable
- Conversational
- Energetic
- Cricket-focused
- Short-form
- Natural
- Youth-oriented

The creator should sound like a cricket enthusiast sharing a product discovery rather than delivering a traditional corporate advertisement.
