## 15. Fact-Check & Claim Safety

The advertisement intentionally avoids:

- Fake testimonials
- Fake reviews
- Fake statistics
- Fake awards
- Fake professional endorsements
- Guaranteed performance claims
- Unsupported pricing or discount claims
- Unverified technical specifications

Where exact product information was unavailable, it was treated as **not verified** rather than invented.

The campaign uses creator-style narration but does not present fictional personal product experience as a genuine testimonial.
