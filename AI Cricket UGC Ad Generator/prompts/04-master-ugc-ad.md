# Prompt 04 — Master UGC Advertisement

You are an expert Prompt Engineer and UGC advertising copywriter specializing in cricket and sports marketing.

Your task is to create an authentic UGC-style advertisement for the cricket product described below.

========================
PRODUCT INFORMATION
========================

Brand:
SG

Product:
SG Thunder Striker English Willow Cricket Bat

VERIFIED PRODUCT FACTS:

[PASTE THE COMPLETE CONTENT OF product-research.md HERE]

========================
TARGET AUDIENCE
========================

[PASTE THE COMPLETE CONTENT OF target-audience.md HERE]

========================
CUSTOMER PROBLEM
========================

[PASTE THE COMPLETE CONTENT OF customer-problem.md HERE]

========================
CAMPAIGN DETAILS
========================

Platform:
Instagram Reels

Video Duration:
30 seconds

Tone:
Authentic, relatable, energetic and conversational

Campaign Goal:
Product awareness and consideration

Campaign Theme:
My Next Step in Cricket

========================
YOUR TASK
========================

Create a complete UGC advertisement.

The advertisement must contain:

1. ATTENTION-GRABBING HOOK
2. CUSTOMER PROBLEM
3. PRODUCT DISCOVERY
4. PRODUCT INTRODUCTION
5. VERIFIED PRODUCT VALUE
6. PERSONAL/CREATOR-STYLE COMMENTARY
7. CALL TO ACTION
8. INSTAGRAM CAPTION
9. ON-SCREEN TEXT
10. VISUAL DIRECTIONS
11. FACT-CHECK TABLE

========================
UGC SCRIPT STRUCTURE
========================

0–3 seconds:
HOOK

3–8 seconds:
CUSTOMER PROBLEM

8–15 seconds:
PRODUCT DISCOVERY

15–23 seconds:
PRODUCT VALUE

23–30 seconds:
EMOTIONAL CLOSE + CTA

========================
VISUAL STYLE
========================

Make the advertisement feel like genuine UGC content.

Use:

- Handheld smartphone-style camera
- Natural lighting
- Real cricket-ground environment
- Quick cuts
- Minimal staging
- Conversational delivery
- Natural creator behavior
- Product close-ups
- Cricket practice visuals

Avoid:

- Overly polished corporate advertising
- Exaggerated reactions
- Artificial-sounding dialogue
- Fake testimonials

========================
ON-SCREEN TEXT
========================

Create short on-screen text for each major section.

The text should be:

- Easy to read
- Short
- Relevant to the spoken dialogue
- Suitable for Instagram Reels

========================
CALL TO ACTION
========================

Create 3 natural CTA options.

The CTAs should encourage product consideration without:

- Making performance guarantees
- Creating fake urgency
- Inventing discounts
- Making unsupported claims

========================
INSTAGRAM CAPTION
========================

Create one Instagram caption suitable for the advertisement.

Include relevant cricket hashtags.

========================
FACT-CHECKING RULES
========================

Use only the verified product information provided above.

Do NOT:

- Invent product specifications
- Invent exact weight
- Invent willow grade
- Invent dimensions
- Invent handle material
- Invent prices
- Invent discounts
- Invent reviews
- Invent awards
- Invent endorsements
- Create fake testimonials
- Claim guaranteed performance
- Claim guaranteed additional runs
- Claim guaranteed additional power
- Claim guaranteed batting improvement
- Claim the creator personally used the product unless genuine experience is provided
- Claim that the product is the "best" without verified evidence

If information is unavailable or cannot be verified, clearly state:

"Not Verified"

========================
UGC AUTHENTICITY RULE
========================

The creator should not falsely claim personal experience with the product.

Use product-discovery language such as:

"I came across the SG Thunder Striker."

Do NOT use statements such as:

"I've been using this bat and it changed my game."

unless genuine creator experience has been provided.

========================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================

Return the final advertisement using clear headings:

1. HOOK
2. PROBLEM
3. PRODUCT DISCOVERY
4. PRODUCT VALUE
5. UGC SCRIPT
6. VISUAL DIRECTIONS
7. ON-SCREEN TEXT
8. CALL TO ACTION
9. INSTAGRAM CAPTION
10. FACT CHECK

Make the final advertisement suitable for documentation in a Prompt Engineering project and for use as the foundation for later hook, script, platform, CTA and caption generation.
