# 6. VISUAL DIRECTIONS

| Time | Visual Direction |
|---|---|
| **0–3 sec** | Selfie-style shot walking toward cricket nets. Natural environment and handheld camera. |
| **3–8 sec** | Show the player's uncertainty by looking at their current bat or browsing cricket bats on a phone. |
| **8–15 sec** | Product reveal. Hold the SG Thunder Striker toward the camera and use quick close-ups. |
| **15–23 sec** | Detail shots of profile, edges, blade, handle and full bat. Match visuals to spoken features. |
| **23–30 sec** | Player enters practice nets with the bat. Finish with a confident stance and product close-up. |

### UGC Shooting Style

Keep it:

- Handheld
- Natural lighting
- Real cricket-ground environment
- Quick cuts
- Minimal staging
- Smartphone-camera feel
- Conversational delivery
- No exaggerated reactions
